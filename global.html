FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What languages does the AI support?

The AI supports 8 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Arabic (with full RTL layout support). It auto-detects each visitor's language on arrival.

支持 8 种语言，含阿拉伯语 RTL 布局，自动识别访客语言。
How do I connect my ad platforms?

Meta CAPI connects via your Pixel ID + Access Token. Google Ads and TikTok conversion send-back are coming soon. Setup takes a few minutes — no developer required.

Meta CAPI 通过 Pixel ID + Access Token 连接，Google / TikTok 回传即将上线。
Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit. We are GDPR / CCPA compliant, and data retention periods are fully configurable by you.

AES-256 加密，符合 GDPR / CCPA，数据保留时长可自定义。
Do I need a technical team?

No. You only need to embed one script tag into your website — the chat widget is live in about 5 minutes. No engineering overhead.

无需技术团队，嵌入一段脚本即可，约 5 分钟上线。
Can the AI handle my product knowledge?

Yes. Upload your product docs, pricelist and FAQ into the knowledge base, and the AI answers accurately based on your own materials — no generic hallucinations about your catalog.

上传产品文档与 FAQ 到知识库，AI 基于您的资料准确作答。
How does conversion tracking work?

When you mark a lead as converted in the inbox, we automatically send the conversion event to your connected ad platforms, so their algorithms keep optimizing toward real buyers — not just clicks.

在收件箱标记成单后，自动把转化事件回传到已连接的广告平台。
What if I need help?

You get a Chinese-speaking support team, reachable via WeChat and email — including help with ad platform setup and go-to-market questions.

中文支持团队，微信 + 邮件均可触达，含广告平台接入协助。
