6AI processingAI 处理

Intent classification, suggested/automated replies, and lead scores are generated by AI (large language models) and may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Only the comment text and minimal context are sent to AI providers—never your OAuth tokens or credentials. You may disable automation, require manual approval, or use rule-only/template replies; the merchant remains responsible for replies sent from its account.意图识别、建议/自动回复及线索评分由 AI（大模型）生成，可能不准确或不恰当。发送给 AI 提供商的仅为评论文本及最小上下文，绝不包含你的 OAuth 令牌或凭据。你可以关闭自动化、改为人工审核，或仅使用规则/模板回复；从你账号发出的回复由商家承担责任。

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256); secrets are stored in environment/secret management, not in client code.OAuth 令牌静态加密（AES-256）；密钥存于环境变量/密钥管理，不写入前端代码。
  • All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS; access controls and least-privilege permissions are enforced.全部流量使用 HTTPS/TLS；实施访问控制与最小权限原则。
  • We never store your social-media passwords—authorization is handled entirely by the platform via OAuth.我们绝不存储你的社交账号密码，授权完全由平台通过 OAuth 完成。

13How to revoke access如何撤销授权

Remove WorkHogee from Instagram / Meta从 Instagram / Meta 移除 WorkHogee

  1. Open the Instagram app → Settings and privacy → Website permissions (or Apps and websites in the Meta Accounts Center).打开 Instagram 应用 → 设置和隐私 → 网站权限（或 Meta 账户中心的应用和网站）。
  2. Find WorkHogee under connected apps/business integrations and choose Remove/Disconnect.在已连接应用/业务集成中找到 WorkHogee，选择移除/断开。

Remove Google sign-in移除 Google 登录

  1. Go to your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.打开 Google 账户 → 安全 → 第三方应用和服务。
  2. Select WorkHogee and choose Delete all connections / Remove access.选择 WorkHogee，点击删除所有连接 / 移除访问权限。

After revocation we stop receiving new events and delete the tokens; remaining data is deleted according to Section 8.撤销后，我们停止接收新事件并删除令牌；其余数据按第 8 节删除。
